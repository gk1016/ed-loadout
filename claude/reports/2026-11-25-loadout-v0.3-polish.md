## Criteria not met

- **Criterion 3, acceptance wording.** The plan's values are in and render as
  computed, but "no FILE TARGET row under the sheet is legible" does not hold in
  `confirm-daylight-desk`: `Graphics/Settings.xml  same bytes` and the bound-device rows
  under the sheet still read. A uniform scrim scales contrast, it does not remove it;
  0.6 leaves dark ink at about 18 on a 108 field. Illegible needs a higher alpha (about
  0.85 or more) or a blur, which the plan did not specify, so I did not pick one.
  Deepfield (0.75) is close to illegible. OBS-022.

## Deviations from the plan

1. **Criterion 4 line numbers.** `ConfirmApply.qml:31` is the `Cell` component (the
   FILE / WRITE / BACKED UP cells), not OPTIONS DIR; `:49` is `Pair` (OPTIONS DIR and
   BACKUP FOLDER). Both are changed as the plan names them; file names and short
   status words fit their columns, so ElideLeft on `Cell` changes nothing visible.
2. **Criterion 5 also changes the GUID text** (`HomePage.qml:265`) beside the kicker at
   `:256`, as `ConfirmApply.qml` `:108` and `:116` do: kicker and value in two colours
   would be wrong by the same rule.
3. **Criterion 6 is conditional on the PROFILES page.** `ProfilesPage.qml` is also
   HOME's left pane, inside a SplitView that lets it go 340-640 wide. Unconditional
   `fillWidth: false; maximumWidth: 430` would leave dead space when the splitter is
   dragged wider. So: `fillWidth: !page.detail`, `maximumWidth: page.detail ? 430 :
   infinity`. HOME behaves as before; PROFILES gets the plan's 430.
4. **Criterion 2 logs the header.** For the report's quoted line, the scripted `apply`
   step in `Main.qml` appends `header="..."` to its `script: confirm open=...` line
   (same commit). `ship.ps1`'s regex on that line still matches.
5. **Criterion 7 runs in harness runs too** (no `--select`): that is what makes the
   restore and installed runs show it. `ship.ps1`'s first-open assertion
   (`profiles=0 selected=none`) is unaffected.
